Clean, Solar Power for Africa’s EV Revolution

Accelerating electric mobility adoption across Nigeria and Sub-Saharan Africa through decentralized solar charging and rapid battery swapping.

Calculate Fuel Savings Find a Charging Hub

The Challenge

High fuel costs, volatile petrol prices, and power grid instability hold back everyday drivers and commercial transport operators.

The Solar Solution

100% off-grid solar charging stations and 2-minute battery swapping hubs that keep electric tricycles, bikes, and vehicles moving efficiently.

Built for Africa's Transport Ecosystem

  • Drivers: Cut daily operating costs by switching from petrol to solar power.
  • Fleet Owners: Lower total cost of ownership and manage assets smoothly.
  • Investors: Support scalable, high-demand clean energy infrastructure.
  • EV Manufacturers: Connect directly to an active charging and swap network.
